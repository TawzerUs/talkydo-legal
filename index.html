Speak. Tasks appear, done.

Tap the globe and just talk — "Call Hasan tomorrow at 9, pay the rent every month, and buy milk." TalkyDo turns your words into organized tasks while you watch.

●Live capture

Task cards appear as you speak, with dates, times, repeats, and priorities parsed automatically. Remove any with a tap, confirm once.

●Private by design

Speech recognition and AI run on your iPhone with Apple Intelligence. No account, no tracking, no server reading your thoughts.

●Meetings & ideas

Record a meeting and get a summary, key points, and action items. Speak a rough idea and get concrete next steps.

●Morning briefing

A daily notification that says what your day actually holds — and reminders you can complete or snooze right from the banner.

●Scan anything

Photograph a whiteboard, a note, or a list, and TalkyDo turns it into tasks you can edit before saving.

●Everywhere you are

iCloud sync, widgets, Control Center, Siri, and two-way Apple Reminders sync keep your list wherever you look.

Your words stay yours. TalkyDo collects nothing — tasks live on your device and in your private iCloud database. That's the whole data policy, and it's written down: read it here.
