TalkyDo Pro — Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 24, 2026

TalkyDo Pro is built so that your data stays yours. This policy describes what the app processes and where it goes.

What we collect

Nothing. TalkyDo Pro has no accounts, no analytics, no tracking, and no advertising. We (Tawzer AppLabs) operate no server that receives your tasks, notes, recordings, or transcripts.

Voice and speech

Speech recognition runs on your device using Apple's speech frameworks. Audio is captured only while a recording screen is open, is used to produce the transcript, and is not stored. Task extraction and meeting summaries are generated on-device by Apple Intelligence when available; otherwise a built-in rule-based parser is used. Your words are not sent to us in either case.

Your content and iCloud

Tasks, projects, and notes are stored on your device. If iCloud sync is enabled (Settings → Data), they are also stored in your private iCloud database, managed by Apple and readable only by your Apple account. Disable the toggle at any time to keep data on-device only.

Optional integrations you control

  • Apple Reminders — if you enable Reminders sync, tasks are mirrored to a "TalkyDo" list in the Apple Reminders app on your device.
  • Photo scanning — images you choose are processed on-device with Apple's Vision framework to extract text. Images are not stored by the app.
  • Premium voices (OpenAI) — if you paste your own OpenAI API key in Settings, the text of assistant replies (never your recordings) is sent to OpenAI to synthesize speech, under OpenAI's privacy policy. The key is stored in your device Keychain. This feature is off unless you configure it.

Permissions

Microphone and speech recognition are used for voice capture; notifications for task reminders and the daily briefing; Reminders access only if you enable sync. Each permission is requested in context and the app works without any of them.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple. We receive no payment details. Manage or cancel in Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions.

Children

TalkyDo Pro is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal information from anyone.
